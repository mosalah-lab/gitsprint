About Global-FX-App

Global-FX-App, built by team coder, lets you browse supported currencies and live rates, look up a single pair, convert amounts with the fee tiers applied, record and review transfers, and check market stats and rate history — all backed by validated, error-checked APIs over a seeded MySQL database.
